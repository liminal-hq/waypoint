| A18  | Accepted (2026-09-30) | **Milestone 0 spikes 1 and 5 results and the rules they set**: 60 Hz scrolling, instant jumps and near-instant selection on a 500 000-row listing in WebKitGTK (Wayland and XWayland), and 500 000 entries scanned in about 0.6 s over a `Channel`. The design must (1) cope with the webview's scroll-height cap of 33 554 428 px, about 1.198 million rows at 28 px, by scaling the scroll position for larger listings; (2) end every stream with an explicit message carrying a count, because channel messages can arrive after the command returns; (3) show placeholder rows during a fling, since at about 700 rows a frame the page fetch cannot keep up; (4) batch scans at about 2 000 to 5 000 entries; and (5) send typed JSON pages, not raw bytes, below a few thousand rows.
