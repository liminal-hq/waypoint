| A18 | Accepted (2026-09-30) | **Milestone 0 spikes 1 and 5 results and the rules they set**: 60 Hz scrolling, instant jumps and near-instant selection on a 500 000-row listing in WebKitGTK (Wayland and XWayland), and 500 000 entries scanned in about 0.6 s over a `Channel`.
